# Solar Power System Design & Simulation with Protection Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project demonstrates the design and simulation of a **grid-connected solar photovoltaic (PV) system** to analyze electrical power generation, DC–AC conversion, and protection mechanisms used in industrial solar plants.

The system is modeled using **MATLAB/Simulink** to study voltage, current, power characteristics, inverter operation, and basic electrical safety concepts.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Study solar PV electrical characteristics
- Understand DC to AC conversion using inverter
- Analyze system efficiency and power flow
- Learn electrical protection and safety in solar systems

---

## ⚙️ System Components
- Solar PV Array
- DC–AC Inverter
- Grid / Load
- Protection Devices (MCB, MCCB, Earthing)

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- MATLAB
- Simulink
- Power Electronics
- Electrical System Modeling

---

## 🔍 Key Features
- V–I and P–V curve analysis of solar PV
- Inverter-based DC–AC conversion
- Power and efficiency evaluation
- Study of over-current and short-circuit protection

---

## 📊 Results
- Verified solar output under varying conditions
- Observed inverter output waveform
- Understood importance of electrical safety and protection

---

## 📚 Learning Outcomes
- Practical knowledge of solar power systems
- Exposure to plant-level electrical concepts
- Understanding of inverter operation and protection
